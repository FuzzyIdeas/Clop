## Features

- **Animated WebP** no longer comes out as a still image
    > Animated GIFs converted to WebP stay animated
    >
    > On Apple Silicon an animated WebP keeps its frames and loop count through optimising, downscaling and cropping, and converts back to GIF
    >
    > Animated WebP is no longer auto-converted to `jpeg` by the *Compatibility* settings

## Fixes

- Optimising an animated GIF no longer flattens it to a single frame when metadata is preserved rather than stripped

## Improvements

- Bundled `ffmpeg` updated to 9.0 on Apple Silicon
